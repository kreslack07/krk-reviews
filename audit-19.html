The 10 checks you get a pass or fail on

  1. Mobile readiness — how the site behaves on the phone in a visitor's hand, which is where most local searches happen.
  2. Fast loading speed — whether the main content arrives quickly enough that an impatient buyer stays.
  3. Secure HTTPS connection — the certificate and whether anything still loads over plain HTTP.
  4. Hidden indexing blocks — a stray noindex tag or robots rule left on after launch, silently telling Google to ignore the site.
  5. Clear page titles and summaries — the title tag and meta description Google previews in results, and whether each is unique.
  6. Logical heading structure — whether the headings read like an outline that answers the customer's search.
  7. Clean internal links — broken links and pages nothing links to, which trap crawlers in dead ends.
  8. Single canonical address — whether the site loads at more than one address and splits its own ranking signal.
  9. Consistent local business signals — name, address, phone and hours matching between the site and the Google Business Profile.
  10. Up-to-date XML sitemap — whether Google has a clean roadmap that lists only live, wanted pages.
