FAQ

What's the cheapest useful option?

A 10-point written check at A$19 AUD, or a free sample report from KRK showing what a paid audit includes.

Are retainers worth it?

Only if the site needs continuous content or technical work. Many small sites need one good fix pass first.

How long until results?

Technical fixes can show in weeks; content and authority take months. Anyone promising page-one in a week is selling hope.

What should an audit include?

Crawl/indexation, Core Web Vitals, titles and meta, schema, internal links, and AI-search readiness — written up with real prices and dates visible.
