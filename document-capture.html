What this does

Any document a person currently retypes or copies by hand — a supplier invoice for the accounts system, a contract for the renewals calendar, a claim form for the register — comes back as structured JSON your system can load directly. Keying in numbers is the job being removed.

Every field carries the line it came from. If a value is not in the document, it is listed as missing. Nothing is guessed, inferred or filled in with a plausible-looking number — the wrong total in an accounts file costs far more than a blank one.

What it handles — and what it admits it cannot

Works today

  • Text-layer PDF (Word exports, accounting and invoicing systems)
  • Word .docx
  • Plain text, Markdown and CSV
  • Invoice, contract and general document profiles
  • Source line recorded for every field
  • Re-run safe: the same document is never processed twice

Declared, not hidden

  • Scanned or photographed pages need the OCR step — flagged, quoted separately
  • PDFs with embedded subset fonts are reported as unreadable rather than wrongly transcribed
  • Handwriting is out of scope
  • Signature-block legal interpretation is out of scope — extraction, not advice
