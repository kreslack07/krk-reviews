Why won't Google show my website in 2026?

Published 2026-11-23 · KRK Reviews · Sydney, Australia

The three most common causes are: the page is not indexed at all (check Search Console's URL inspection), the robots.txt or a noindex tag blocks it, or Google considers the content thin or duplicated. Each has a specific fix, and most small-business sites have at least one of the three.

FAQ

How do I check if my site is indexed?

In Google Search Console, use URL inspection on the exact page. If it says 'not indexed', submit it after fixing any blocking issue.

Does llms.txt help my site rank?

No evidence supports that. Google states no special AI file or markup is required; llms.txt is a proposal no engine documents reading.

Could my host or platform be the problem?

Sometimes — JavaScript-only rendering, blocked crawlers in robots.txt, or a server returning 5xx errors. A technical crawl finds these in minutes.
