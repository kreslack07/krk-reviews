🚀 Digital Products Launch Checklist

Your step-by-step roadmap to building a profitable digital products business in 2026

Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1-2)

Define Your Niche & Audience

Identify a specific problem you solve for a specific audience. The narrower, the better for SEO and conversions.

Register Business & ABN

Australian sole trader ABN registration (free). Required for Stripe, Wise, and tax compliance.

Set Up Banking & Payments

Wise Business multi-currency account + Stripe for cards. Connect Stripe payouts to Wise.

Phase 2: Product Creation (Week 2-4)

Create First Digital Product

Solve ONE problem completely. PDF, Notion template, spreadsheet, or code bundle. Price $19-49.

Build Product Delivery System

Gumroad (easiest start) or own site + Stripe + webhook fulfillment. Test end-to-end purchase.

Create Product Assets

Cover image (1600x1200), preview images, sales page copy, FAQ, license terms.

Phase 3: Traffic & Sales (Week 4-8)

Launch Review/Comparison Site

SEO-optimized comparison pages targeting buyer-intent keywords. Netlify + GitHub for free hosting.

Set Up Email Capture

Lead magnet (this checklist) → Mailchimp/ConvertKit → Welcome sequence → Pitch sequence.

Start Content Marketing

Pinterest pins, YouTube Shorts, Reddit answers, Quora answers — all linking to review pages.

Join Affiliate Programs

Amazon Associates (kreslack07-22), software affiliates, course affiliates. Add to review pages.

Phase 4: Scale (Month 3+)

Create Product Ladder

Free → $27 → $97 → $297 → $997. Each product solves the "next problem" after the previous.

Automate Fulfillment & Support

Webhooks → Notion/Google Sheets → Auto-delivery. Templates for common support questions.

Reinvest Profits Into Ads

Start with $10/day Pinterest/Meta retargeting. Scale winners. Track CAC < 30% of LTV.
