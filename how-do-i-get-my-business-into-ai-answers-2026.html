FAQ

Do I need special schema for AI Overviews?

No. Google states pages must simply be indexed and eligible for a snippet; no AI-specific markup is required.

Does llms.txt help?

Evidence so far says it does not move citations; it doesn't harm either. Low priority compared to real answer-first content.

How is it measured?

Ask 10–20 real buyer questions in each assistant monthly, signed out, and record whether your business is cited and described accurately.
